# Insights

## What the structure buys

Maudlin's trilemma turns a vague worry into a map. The incompatibility is not
a matter of taste: in the exact toy model (script 01), completeness + linearity
yield a detector in two orthogonal reading states — two nonzero pointer
branches — and single outcomes demand exactly one. The contradiction is
re-derivable in a subprocess in milliseconds, and its pure-logic skeleton is a
three-line Lean theorem. Once the incompatibility is exact, the interpretation
landscape becomes a truth table: 7 admissible valuations, 1 excluded, and
three of the admissible ones are *doors* — inhabited by Bohm (011), collapse
(101), and many-worlds (110).

## The prices are asymmetric in practice, symmetric in logic

Logic treats the three doors evenly: each pair of claims is consistent, each
sacrifice is one claim. Physics does not. The collapse door pays in tested
dynamics (script 03 shows how the GRW parameter is engineered: one particle
waits 10¹⁶ s, a dust grain collapses in 10⁻⁷ s — the amplification is the
whole trick). The Bohm door pays in ontology (positions everywhere) and
nonlocality. The many-worlds door pays in outcomes-and-probability — the one
price that resists formalization: the study's `everett_emergence_gap` is a
*deliberately unproven* Lean theorem, because the missing step (Deutsch–Wallace
decision theory, self-locating uncertainty) is exactly what the literature
still argues about. A sorry can be honest documentation of an open problem.

## The structure is not quantum-specific

The fluidic fork (script 06, docs/06) realizes the same logic with pipes: a
symmetric fork feeding a latch cannot produce a selection (a tie is not an
outcome), and adding one extra variable ε fixes it deterministically. What
quantum theory adds is not the three-door structure but the constraint that
ε-variables must be nonlocal (Bell). Cross-domain status: the arithmetic is
verified; the analogy is conjectural — and the cent ledger says so.

## The method's value-add over a good essay

Every claim here is either (a) re-derived twice by disjoint paths in isolated
subprocesses, (b) formalized in Lean where possible, (c) named-as-gap where
not, or (d) named-as-premise. The essay-level content of docs/01 and docs/04
is thereby pinned: if any of it drifts from the verified core, the
`KEY=VALUE` outputs and the SORRY_LEDGER are the fixed points to check against.

## One honest caveat

The study's framing (three claims as "Maudlin 1995") is a conversational
premise carried in docs/05/docs/08 — a claim about a text, held as an
axiom-class premise, not a derivation. The derivations stand on their own:
given the three claims and the standard linear measurement interaction, all
structural results follow exactly.
