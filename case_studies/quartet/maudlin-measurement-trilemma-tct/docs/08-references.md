## Premise note

The specific three-claim formulation (completeness / linearity / outcomes) is
carried in this study as a conversational premise attributed to Maudlin
(1995), per the study brief; the reconstruction is standard in the secondary
literature as "Maudlin's trilemma" (e.g. Halvorson's foundations reading
list). All structural claims about the trilemma (the incompatibility, the
truth table, the three doors) are re-derived exactly in this repository and
do not depend on the premise note.
