# Insights

## The question decomposes into a theorem and a refusal

"How can c be derived computationally?" has a clean two-part answer that no
single framing delivers:

- **Structure is derivable — exactly, computationally, in several independent
  ways.** An invariant maximum speed emerges from any faithful discrete
  dynamics (integer wave scheme: edge speed exactly 1/step; Clifford circuits:
  support growth ≤ 1/layer), is rigorously bounded in microscopic Hamiltonians
  (LR: v = 2Ja), and follows from symmetry + causality alone (Ignatowski:
  κ ≥ 0). The *cone* is computation's to give.
- **The value is not derivable — it is measured (α) or defined (SI).** The
  one dimensionless input enters at the bottom; everything numeric is either
  bookkeeping (script 01: `DEFINITIONAL=1`) or bookkeeping-given-measurement
  (script 06: 1/α exactly from α). The value boundary is recorded as a
  *deliberate Lean sorry*, not swept under prose.

The intellectual gain of the TCT treatment: the question stops being
mystical-adjacent and becomes a decomposition — derivable / definable /
measured / refused — each part witnessed or named.

## Three independent cones agree — that is the deep part

The wave scheme, the Clifford circuit, and the Lieb–Robinson bound are
different mathematics (PDE numerics, finite group theory, operator algebras)
yet their cones agree exactly where they overlap (`FRAMEWORKS_MATCH=1`,
`BOUND_TIGHT=1` at J = 1/2). When three unrelated formalisms compute the same
object and it survives double-path verification, that object is *structure*,
not artifact. This is the strongest result the study can honestly offer: not
"we derived 299,792,458" but "we derived what a light cone is, three ways,
and they coincide".

## Refusals are results

The naive claim is refused exactly once, with a named reason
(`WOULD_BE_GUESS`), and the refusal is *load-bearing*: it separates the
Eddington-lineage numerology class — post-hoc fits to measured constants —
from the licensed derivations. The acceptance bar is written down (fix α
*and* predict independent constants parameter-free), so future claims can be
graded against a rule, not a mood. The companion studies' habits carry over:
the Maudlin study's deliberate `sorry` (Everett emergence) and this study's
(`alpha_from_premises`) serve the same function — the ledger shows what the
field actually owes.

## Where the question pushed back (and what we learned)

- **Circularity**: the modern c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) computation is circular post-2019
  — the historically honest legs (unit-ratio vs. optics) had to be used
  instead. Lesson: check the metrological lineage before "deriving" a
  constant from constants.
- **Definitions wearing derivation's clothes**: computing a defined number is
  not deriving it; stamping `DEFINITIONAL=1` makes the category error
  impossible to repeat inside the study.
- **Emergent-c overclaiming**: every lattice/hypergraph/analog program derives
  *a* cone; every one of them fits the value. Keeping those two columns apart
  is the whole discipline.

## The trilogy's arc

Three studies, one method: Maudlin (interpretation trilemma → three doors),
physical isomorphisms (five gains + one refusal of complexity-class miracles),
speed of light (structure derivable + value measured + one deliberate
boundary). In each, the answer that survives verification is a *map of what
is knowable how* — which is what the questioner was actually asking.
