# Insights

## "First principles" is a stratified commodity

The question sounds like one request; the study shows it is five, and the
strata are where the fun is. D constants (π, e, φ, √2) pay out in exact
certificates — three independent π derivations agreeing to 25 digits is
*satisfying* in a way that a single formula never is. T constants (ln 2,
ζ(2), ζ(4)) need one theorem bolted onto the machinery. O constants (γ, ζ(5),
Catalan's closure) are the celebrities: fully computable, famously unclaimed.
U (Ω) is the vertigo case — definable, digits forever dark. P (α) is where
the trilogy's refusal stands untouched. The coastline D→T→O→U→P *is* the
answer to the question.

## Certificates are the difference between derivation and numerology

Every interval in this study carries a proof that it is tight: alternating
remainders, telescoping tails, Pell certificates, Cassini identities. That is
exactly what the Eddington-lineage "derivations" of 1/137 lack — they match
digits; ours match *bounds*. The guardrail isn't anti-fun; it is what makes
the fun honest. (And the assert layer caught four of my own defects mid-build
— two of them in the *witness method itself*, the truncation-anchor mistake.
Meta-lesson: verify your verification method.)

## The two walls rhyme with the trilogy

γ and α are both "famous numbers nobody can derive" — but they are different
kinds of wall. γ is mathematical: a closed form or irrationality proof would
be a celebrated theorem *within* our framework. α is physical: no framework
produces it from nothing. Keeping both as deliberate `sorry`s — with gap text
that says which kind of wall each is — turns the ledger into a map of where
mathematics ends and measurement begins. Study 3's `alpha_from_premises` and
this study's `gamma_rational_target` now sit side by side: two named holes,
one earned, one inherited.

## Ω is the punchline

The question said "derived from first principles" — and Chaitin's Ω is
exactly that: a first-principles definition whose digits no algorithm can
print. It is the constant that proves "derivable" and "computable" are
different words. Class U earns its place in the catalog by being the
exception that sharpens every other row.

## The trilogy's arc, completed

Four studies, one method: Maudlin's trilemma (interpretations pay), physical
isomorphisms (physics buys speed but not complexity classes), the speed of
light (structure derivable, value measured), and now the constants (five
strata of derivability, two famous walls). In each, the verified result is
not a formula but a *map of what is knowable how* — with every claim either
double-path proved, axiom-named, gap-named, or refused. That is what the
questions were all along really asking for.
